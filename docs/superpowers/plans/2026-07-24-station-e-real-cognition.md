## Flagged plan-level decisions (founder review — flagged, not silent)

1. **The wind-up commits as a ruled `AttributeChanged` on the acting NPC via `apply_ruled_event` (origin `telegraph`).** Six-types-only forbids a `telegraph` event type; `AttributeChanged` on self ("Jonas pushes off the bar, moving to cut in") is the honest fit, and `apply_ruled_event` already does correct fan-out + subject rows. This is engine clerk work recording the mind's telegraphed intent — the ACT itself is adjudicated later in the combined ruling, so always-adjudicated is not bypassed.
2. **`adjudicate` loses its single `actorID`: attempts become `ActorAttempt{ActorID, Attempt}` pairs.** The combined ruling (RULINGS-2026-07-23 §9) judges attempts from DIFFERENT actors; attribution must ride each attempt.
3. **"Public moment" and "private record" get mechanical definitions:** public = a source event every present actor holds a perception of, rendered with the modal (most common) content among present holders; private = any record the NPC holds that fails that test. A per-receiver variant holder in the batch loses her sharper read for that action — dull, never a leak (§5's sanctioned failure direction).
4. **`personality_core` (traits + manner + malleability) rides shared prompts; therefore secrets may NEVER live in core traits.** The DRAFT's "core-adjacent knowledge (`knows_kade_as`)" moves INTO Mara's private perception record. A core is who she is in the room; a secret is what she knows — only the latter is walled.
5. **Station E reads cores into cognition prompts minimally (name + traits + manner + malleability).** Station H1's full core→prefix assembly (backstory-event retrieval, §10 subject-triggered memory) supersedes this read; H's magnitude/threshold/pool machinery is untouched here.
6. **Tension review on cognition steps is NOT in this station** (the map's E row doesn't carry it; npc_attempts/1 has no tension slot and stays frozen). Named deferral — founder objections welcome now, not after.
7. **`held_outcome` rows are written by Go in the same pgx tx as the wind-up commit.** They are loop state, not canon — D-1 (SQL-only canon writes) is untouched; canon still goes through `apply_ruled_event`/`apply_event`.
