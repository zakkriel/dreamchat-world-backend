# world_model/5 — the reader-half correction

v4 fixed the author's half against evidence. **The reader's half had never been run.** The first round
that ran it stopped on its first finding, and v5 is that finding and nothing else.

Delta over v4. Everything in v3 and v4 holds unless changed here. **One change.**

---

## 1. What the round found

Two builders were handed one v4 document (`R_input_grelda.md`), the reader obligations of `SCHEMA-v3.md`
§4, and a sheet of concrete play questions. Each was told to build and to commit to one answer. Neither
was told to look at `channel.conceals`, neither was told the other existed, and neither was asked to
audit anything.

Both rendered an entity's `hiding` string as **public**:

> `la Ochenta y Tres` — **hiding EXPOSED**: "It has been running down for want of people, not grain, for
> sixty years, and the knocking is the last thing it can still do."
> — `R_grelda_aborted_b1.md`

> the `hiding` string *"It has been running down for want of people, not grain, for sixty years, and the
> knocking is the last thing it can still do."* exposed
> — `R_grelda_aborted_b2.md`

Both cited the same authority: `conceals: "none"` plus the §4 row. They were **right to**. The round was
aborted at that question, unscored, by a condition written before it ran.

**The defect.** `SCHEMA-v3.md:121` read:

> | `channel.conceals` | what identity is withheld; `none` renders present entities' `pursuing` and `hiding` to all receivers |

The field's own first clause scopes it to **identity**. The obligation then also discloses **intent and
secrets**. In any world whose ordinary senses conceal nothing — `conceals: "none"` on sight and speech,
which is the normal case, and the case in every document written so far — every `hiding` string becomes
public to everyone present. That deletes what **O4** exists to protect: *"with nothing withheld
anywhere, every conversation is exhaustive and discovery is impossible."* The fixture has four `hiding`
strings; the obligation published three of them the moment a player walked into the district that
contains them, and the fourth as soon as he crossed one passage.

O4 obliged the author to withhold. §4 obliged the builder to publish. A contract cannot hold both.

---

## 2. The change — `channel.conceals` becomes two obligations

The one field was doing two jobs. Separate them. In `SCHEMA-v3.md` §4 "Knowing", replace the single
`channel.conceals` row with:

| Author writes | Builder must derive |
|---|---|
| `channel.conceals` | **what identity is withheld from a receiver.** `none` means every receiver learns which entity emitted, and nothing further |
| — | a channel **never** discloses an entity's `hiding`. An interior becomes knowable only through an act the entity performs, an `indicator` that reads it, or a `trace` it leaves |

The second row is an obligation with no authored key, which is why it was missing: nothing in the
document prompts it. That is exactly the class of reader obligation only a reader test can find.

**`pursuing` is not moved into the second row.** A want is legitimately visible — but it is disclosed by
the entity's `doing`, an ordinary observation of behaviour, not by a channel's silence about identity.
The Eighty-Three's `doing` is *"knocking the same figure into the floor every night"*; anyone on the
climb can see it wants something. Its `hiding` is *why*, and that is not on the channel. This is the
distinction the original row collapsed, and collapsing it is what made the row look right.

Reader-obligation count: **23 → 24**. (`SCHEMA-v3.md`'s own summary table at line 19 says 21; that count
was already wrong — §4 has 5 + 4 + 5 + 8 + 1 = 23 rows. Use the rows.)

---

## 3. Why this is a delta and not an edit

v3 and v4 are the record of what died and why. Editing `SCHEMA-v3.md:121` in place would destroy the
constraint that death represents — that a field named for one job was silently given a second, and that
no author-side round could catch it because no author-side round reads.

## 4. What v5 does not change

Nothing. No other row, no obligation, no refusal, no facet. The round earned exactly one change and it
is above.

**The re-run confirms the fix and earns nothing further.** Three fresh blind builders, given the two rows
above, all three disclosed only emitter identity and refused **both** `hiding` and `pursuing` — one of
them citing the second row's carrier list verbatim. `R_score_grelda.md` §1 Q18, §5, and §9 item 1.

**What that same re-run found next is not a change to this row — it is a gap beside it**, and it is
recorded rather than patched here: the second row names three carriers (an act, an `indicator`, a
`trace`) and does not order them or say whether they compose; and more broadly, `medium`, `affords`,
`resists`, `alters`, `senses` and `confer` — six authored keys bearing on whether a receiver receives
anything at all — have **no reader obligation whatsoever**. A blind seat holding only the obligation
table and one document named that hole independently, and proposed one row for it: a `receipt` obligation
over the *(emitter, receiver)* pair. See `R_score_grelda.md` §8.

That is a v6 question. It is larger than one row, it needs its own evidence, and inventing it here would
repeat the mistake v3 made — specifying a composition from principle before any reading showed what it
has to compose.
