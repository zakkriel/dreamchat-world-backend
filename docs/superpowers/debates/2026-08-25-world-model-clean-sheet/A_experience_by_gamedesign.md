# A — Arrival, experience, and shared worlds

Each topic: what it is · *Author* / *Engine* · **Beat** it is felt · **+P2** what a second player changes.
No beat, no topic. No +P2, it is single-player thinking that will need rewriting.

## 1. The model

### Ground — what exists and where

**Extent & nesting.** Places contain places — a tree, not a room list. *Author:* what sits inside what, a
size class. *Engine:* position, distance, travel time. **Beat 1:** there is a beyond. **+P2:** nesting is
what makes "far" mean the same to two people.

**Passage.** A boundary carries properties, a mover carries properties, crossing is a comparison.
*Author:* what a boundary obstructs. *Engine:* the comparison and the refusal. **Beat 2:** the world says
no, and the reason is a property you can work on. **+P2:** one player opens a way permanently; the other
inherits it open, never knowing it was shut.

**Ambient demand.** A place can be hostile to *staying*, not just entering. *Author:* what it demands.
*Engine:* compare; block or impose a condition. **Beat 2-3:** a place you can enter but not remain in.
**+P2:** the strongest cooperation primitive — one player makes a place habitable for another.

**Things.** Identity, bulk, capacity, condition, location; possession is physical fact, never permission.
*Author:* what it is, a bulk class, what it holds. *Engine:* weight, volume, whether you can lift it.
**Beat 2:** the thing you cannot simply pick up. **+P2:** two claimants, one object — the engine blocks
rather than arbitrates, so it is played out.

**Dependency.** Remove this and something elsewhere degrades. *Author:* what depends on what. *Engine:*
propagation and its delay. **Beat 4-5:** you learn what you took mattered. **+P2:** the load-bearing
multiplayer topic — the only clean way one player changes a place another has never seen.

### Bodies — how people differ physically

**Capability.** How a body moves, bears, endures. *Author:* named ways of moving, a class per body.
*Engine:* speed, load, duration. **Beat 1:** the same stair is nothing to you, impossible beside you.
**+P2:** asymmetric capability is why a party exists.

**Senses & channels.** A fact arrives by a channel or not at all. *Author:* the channels, and who is acute
or absent on each. *Engine:* who could have perceived an event. **Beat 1:** you hear what you cannot see.
**+P2:** the cleanest generator of divergence — one heard it, one didn't, neither knows.

**Conditions.** Transient states from events that alter capability. *Author:* the conditions and what they
hinder. *Engine:* modification, duration, decay. **Beat 3:** consequence you carry out of the room.
**+P2:** transferable harm — one causes, another wears.

### Minds — who is here

**Disposition.** How a person acts, in their manner. *Author:* dispositions and how each shows. *Engine:*
nothing; rendered, not computed. **Every beat:** people are distinguishable. **+P2:** unchanged, and that
is the point — a person is not a function of any one player.

**Intention & opposition.** What each person is doing now, wants next, and one present pair whose
intentions cannot both be satisfied. *Author:* all three. *Engine:* who acts unbidden. **Beat 1** you
interrupt something; **2-5** people act unaddressed. **+P2:** without authored opposition the room's only
motion is players talking to each other.

**Obligation & allegiance.** What a person owes, and to whom. *Author:* the duty and its holder. *Engine:*
nothing; it bounds what an intention may become. **Beat 3:** refused for a reason unrelated to you.
**+P2:** an obligation may name a player — the world can owe you.

**Regard.** Directed, asymmetric, per-pair. *Author:* how A holds B, per direction. *Engine:* nothing.
**Beat 2:** you are someone's rival before doing anything. **+P2:** regard toward players must accrue in
play, or a returning player is a stranger forever.

### Knowing — the real substance

**Belief with provenance.** Every held fact names the event it came from and the channel it arrived by.
*Author:* who believes what, and how. *Engine:* who learns next; refusal of a fact with no path.
**Beat 1:** you know one thing — that you walked in. **+P2:** there is no "the players know", only
per-person. That is the architecture.

**Misbelief & staleness.** A belief may be wrong, or true-then and false-now, until something corrects it.
*Author:* the false belief and what makes it plausible. *Engine:* never reconciling it silently.
**Beat 4:** someone acts confidently on what you know is untrue. **+P2:** misbelief becomes the default —
most of what you know about another player is secondhand.

**Naming & recognition.** Names are earned per person; identity is a mapping. *Author:* the true name and
how a stranger appears. *Engine:* per-viewer rendering. **Beat 1:** everyone is a descriptor. **+P2:** you
can share a room for an hour without a name; being introduced is an event.

**Transmission.** How a fact travels with nobody present: telling, overhearing, evidence, inference.
*Author:* who talks to whom, and this world's rumour paths. *Engine:* propagation, distortion, delay.
**Beat 5:** you hear a version of something you did. **+P2:** the only route by which B learns of A.

### Rule — what this world will not permit

**Norms.** Constraints on acts: who is bound, what is forbidden or required; enforcement by persons, never
the gate. *Author:* the constraint and its binding. *Engine:* nothing decides guilt. **Beat 2:** an NPC
refuses something physically possible. **+P2:** players enforce on each other — the social layer for free.

**World-particular law.** What matter does *here* that it does not elsewhere, as properties compared.
*Author:* the property and what carries it. *Engine:* the comparison. **Beat 2-3:** the strangeness is
usable, not narrated. **+P2:** the first to work it out can teach it, or lie about it.

### Motion — the world without you

**Cycles & scheduled futures.** Things that recur on a period; things about to happen once. *Author:* the
event, a period or imminence class, where it lands. *Engine:* clock, re-arm, firing. **Beat 3-5:** the
world moves while you talk. **+P2:** a cycle is the only event several players reliably share — the spine
of a shared calendar.

**Accumulation & thresholds.** Quantities that build until they tip: pressure, debt, suspicion, wear.
*Author:* what accumulates and what tipping looks like. *Engine:* counting and threshold. **Beat 5+:** the
reason to return tomorrow. **+P2:** world-scoped, so one player raises the stakes for everyone.

**Decay, absence & long spans.** Things degrade unattended; places change unwatched. *Author:* what needs
attention and what neglect looks like. *Engine:* lazy computation of the unwatched interval, and a summary
a returning person could have learned. **Session 2, beat 1:** you come back and it moved. **+P2:** without
it the world changes only where someone stands.

### Shared — arriving into somebody else's world

**Arrival, plural and staggered.** A newcomer enters at any point in the world's life, knowing nothing,
into a world others have altered. *Author:* premises — situations, never interiors. *Engine:* the
ignorance, and a stranger-view of an already-changed place. **Beat 1.** **+P2:** "the opening state" stops
existing.

**Presence.** Who is here now; the beat is shared. *Author:* nothing. *Engine:* co-location, and one
resolution both players live with. **Beat 1:** someone is here who is not a character.

**Attribution & witness.** What can be pinned on whom, and who saw it. *Author:* nothing. *Engine:*
derived from who perceived what. **Beat 4:** you are accused, rightly or not. **+P2:** the crime-and-trust
layer, free once belief has provenance.

## 3. The three hardest to get right

**Misbelief that stays wrong.** An LLM pipeline pulls toward coherence, and a false belief is an
incoherence the next generation quietly repairs. Sella must go on believing the cold did it, and act on
it, across sessions, in front of a player holding evidence. Making a wrong thing persist is harder than
making a right thing propagate — and it is where most of the drama lives.

**Shared time with staggered arrival.** Two players in different places must both act, in one canon, on
one clock, neither waiting — and a third arrives mid-stride into an altered world. Every naive answer
(turn order, one global tick, per-player instances) freezes someone or forks the world. The topic list
is easy by comparison; this is the engineering.

**Unwatched motion.** Cycles, decay and accumulation must run where nobody looks, or the world changes
only under observation. But cheaply, and *without invention*: "what happened here while you were gone"
must be derived, not narrated. Wrong one way and the world is a diorama; wrong the other and it drifts
into nonsense nobody authored.

## 4. What multiplayer breaks or changes

It removes the idea of *the* player, and with it three assumptions.

**There is no opening state.** "Before anyone came" is a fact about tick zero, not something anyone
experiences. Every arrival needs its own manufactured ignorance against a world already worked over,
including a stranger-view of a place another player changed. Nothing in the model may be expressed as "at
the start", because the model must seat a newcomer at any age of the world.

**Knowledge stops being a tree.** Per-person and only per-person: no global "known", no world-level truth
anyone can query. Two players in one room diverge immediately by channel — one heard it, one did not, and
neither can see the other's state. Transmission becomes the only route by which one player's actions reach
another, so distortion and delay are the normal case, not garnish.

**Accumulation goes world-scoped, and that is the point.** One player can push a threshold for everybody:
the best property here — the reason a shared world beats several private ones — and the most dangerous,
since one person can degrade a place others rely on. Dependency and ambient demand exist to make that
legible: you can *see* that the blower feeds the Dry Side, so taking it is a choice about other people
rather than an accident.

Three concrete consequences. **Regard must accrue toward players**, or a returning player is permanently
a stranger. **Absence is a state, not a disappearance** — a logged-off player is a body others can find,
rob, or wait for; if characters vanish with their players, the world is time-shared, not shared. And
**possession conflict must block, not arbitrate**: two players reaching for one thing is a scene, not a
race condition.
