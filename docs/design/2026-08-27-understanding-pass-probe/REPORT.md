# Probe report — the understanding pass hand-run on Los Andantes tier 1

One 400-word brief (Los Andantes, tier 1 — a world never encoded at any tier) went into the
understanding pass defined by the recovered genesis design. Out came one inferred world identity,
judged twice by agents that never saw each other's work. This report is the founder's read: four
judgements with their evidence, the measured rule count, and one decision only the founder can make.

**The headline: the pass grasped the world.** It derived 10 of 13 negative-canon entries it was never
shown, every one of the 11 pressures the richer tiers state derives from its single bargain, and an
isolated judge found only 3 of its twenty daily-life answers liftable into another world. The
defects found are real but narrow: one substantive contradiction, and two distinct protective rules
it failed to infer.

## How to read the numbers

The twenty answers do not have to be uniquely creative — a bland identity is fine when the world's
own intention is bland (a flat world is a legitimate outcome, design §6.1). The failure this probe
looks for is a rich identity the pass could not grasp. Los Andantes tier 1 is rich — fixed migratory
routes, a weight limit that sickens the ground, passage only at calculable convergences, a dead
Andante sinking in six days — so generic answers HERE would read as failure to grasp, not as a plain
world. They did not materialize: 17 of 20 answers are world-specific on their face.

## Judgement 2 — The twenty answers and their verdicts

`could exist elsewhere`: **3 of 20** (answers 9, 14, 19). The isolated judge saw ONLY the twenty
answers — no brief, no tiers, no design — so a verdict of `world-specific` means the answer carries
its world on its face. Reproduced verbatim from `U_andantes_criterion.md` (the answers are identity
section 12, unedited), so ten seconds per answer is all a re-judgement costs:

# Judgement

1. **Who feeds people:** Cultivo en terrazas de sedimento sobre el lomo y pesca desde el borde; toda cosecha se pesa, porque el grano almacenado cuenta contra el límite del Andante.
   `world-specific` — Grain counting against a weight limit of a living "Andante" only makes sense on a colossal walking creature.

2. **Who repairs and makes things:** Se repara mucho y se fabrica poco: cada objeto nuevo son kilos con licencia, así que el oficio dominante es el remendón, y desmontar vale tanto como construir.
   `world-specific` — Manufacturing licensed by kilograms presupposes the world's mass-limit regime.

3. **Who moves goods:** Dentro del Andante, porteadores por senda dorsal; entre Andantes, solo Convergentes en fecha o la Flota Muerta fuera de ella. Un flete tiene fecha de siglo, no de semana.
   `world-specific` — Dorsal paths, Convergence dates, and century-long freight are unique to wandering Andantes.

4. **The sick and the dead:** Los muertos van al agua por el borde: enterrarlos sería cargarlos. A los enfermos se les cuida en Cuenca; nadie pesa a un moribundo, por decencia, aunque el Gremio podría.
   `world-specific` — Burial-as-weight-burden and weighing the dying depend on the mass-limit premise.

5. **Raising and teaching the young:** Los niños aprenden primero el mapa anatómico de su Andante y las Tablas de Convergencia — saben antes qué año viene Quinta que leer de corrido.
   `world-specific` — Anatomical maps of one's Andante and Convergence Tables are unliftable specifics.

6. **Where people with nothing sleep:** En Cola Baja, el tercio posterior, donde el movimiento del paso es peor y nadie con licencia quiere estar; ser pobre es vivir donde el suelo se mueve más.
   `world-specific` — Poverty defined by living where the creature's gait shakes most is unique to this world.

7. **What exchange is:** Todo precio tiene dos componentes: valor y peso. Un objeto pesado vale menos de lo que cuesta, y el dinero ideal es lo que no pesa: deuda, licencia, fecha de pasaje.
   `world-specific` — Dual value-weight pricing follows directly from the carrying-capacity premise.

8. **Pleasure:** Se sube al Alto Omóplato a ver caminar el horizonte; en años de Convergencia, la fiesta dura lo que el cruce, y los nacidos entonces llevan el nombre del Andante visitante.
   `world-specific` — Watching the horizon walk from the "High Shoulder-blade" is inseparable from a walking beast.

9. **How ordinary people settle a dispute:** Por registro: quien tiene el asiento más antiguo en el libro correspondiente gana. Discutir sin papel es cosa de Cola Baja.
   `could exist elsewhere` — Disputes settled by oldest registry entry could exist in any bureaucratic society (place name aside, the mechanism is generic).

10. **What happens to the old:** Los viejos ceden sus kilos: reducen casa, regalan objetos, y morir "ligero" es morir bien. Un viejo con casa grande es un escándalo silencioso.
    `world-specific` — Dying "light" as virtue is tied to the kilo-limit ethic of the Andante.

11. **Waste:** Todo residuo va por el borde al mar — es el único acto en que aligerar es virtud pública, y los distritos compiten en cuánto tiran.
    `world-specific` — Lightening as public virtue and competitive dumping off the edge presuppose the weight economy.

12. **Ordinary news:** El parte mensual es la única noticia con firma; lo demás es rumor de porteador. Del resto del mundo no se sabe nada entre Convergencias, y a nadie le parece raro.
    `world-specific` — Total isolation between Convergences ties news to the world's rare-encounter structure.

13. **A normal day's work:** Pesar, anotar, remendar, cultivar en terraza; media ciudad trabaja directa o indirectamente para uno de los dos libros: el Registro de Peso o el parte.
    `world-specific` — Half the city serving the Weight Register depends on the mass-accounting regime.

14. **Fear unrelated to the bargain:** Las tormentas: en un océano sin costa, el viento cruza el lomo sin nada que lo pare, y lo que no está amarrado se va al agua.
    `could exist elsewhere` — Fear of storms sweeping things overboard fits any ship, raft city, or island in an open ocean.

15. **What people find funny:** El humor es de báscula: llamar "obra con licencia" a un gordo, exagerar el peso de todo, fingir auscultar a un borracho y declararlo "en observación".
    `world-specific` — Scale-humor and mock-auscultation joke on the weight-license and living-beast premises.

16. **What marks status:** Vivir en zona alta y estable (Alto Omóplato), tener asientos antiguos en los libros, y llevar nombre de Convergencia — nacido en año de encuentro.
    `world-specific` — Status via Convergence-birth names and the Shoulder-blade district are world-bound.

17. **What children are warned about:** Los Espiráculos —cuatrocientos metros que no se cruzan—, el borde en tormenta, y preguntar la edad del Andante delante de mayores.
    `world-specific` — Blowholes (Espiráculos) and the taboo of asking the Andante's age only exist on a living creature.

18. **What a stranger is:** Alguien de otro Andante, reconocible al hablar; un extranjero es literalmente alguien de otro mundo, visto como mucho una vez por generación, y su visita tiene fecha de fin.
    `world-specific` — Strangers seen once a generation with an end-dated visit follow from the Convergence structure.

19. **What privacy means:** Lo privado es lo no registrado. Como todo objeto y obra consta en un libro, la intimidad real es lo que no pesa: lo dicho, lo oído, lo que uno auscultó y no firmó.
    `could exist elsewhere` — Privacy as the unregistered could hold in any total-registry surveillance society; "what doesn't weigh" is a flavor, not a dependency.

20. **What counts as clean and dirty:** Limpio es ligero y asentado en registro; sucio es el peso sin licencia, la carga escondida. La mugre moral de este mundo se mide en kilos no declarados.
    `world-specific` — Moral dirt measured in undeclared kilos hinges on the weight-license premise.

## Judgement 3 — Does the bargain generate the world's actual pressures?

Yes — all 11 of them. The bargain under test: *"Tu suelo está vivo y puede morir; por lo tanto todo
lo que construyes, cargas o callas es una apuesta sobre la salud de algo que no puedes dirigir ni
curar, y solo puedes escuchar."* For every pressure tiers 2/3 state — geography-as-calendar,
diagnosis without cure, the political monthly report, falsification-or-error, the administrative
author of the overload, the immovable double deadline, the refuge with its own weight limit, the
slow political catastrophe, the oversold passage market, the system-wide open bet, the single-case
statistics — the scorer derived it from that one bargain as a face (`U_andantes_score.md` Finding 3,
one numbered derivation per pressure, each with the tier line). No pressure needed a second bargain;
none exposed a wrong altitude. The design's one-world-or-two test (§3.1) passes. The scorer flags
one weak derivation honestly: pressure 11 (single-case statistics) derives, but it is also the one
pressure the identity never voices — the same gap as refusal row 13.

## Judgement 4 — Contradictions

Two, from the scorer's Finding 1, each with the quoted line:

1. **Substantive.** The identity's Departure says *"la muerte de Octavo es historia registrada en
   una tabla, no trauma fundacional"*. Both tiers state the opposite — tier2:41: *"El caso del
   Octavo es la referencia histórica de todo el sistema institucional actual … la Flota Muerta se
   fundó a partir de sus supervivientes"*; tier3:52: *"el origen de toda la arquitectura
   institucional vigente."* The pass read the brief's dry register as emotional flatness; the richer
   tiers say the dryness IS the trauma response.
2. **Minor.** The identity's recorded question 2 says *"sus ciudades se hundieron"* (plural); the
   tiers name one city, Ruma (tier2:41, tier3:52). It sits inside a would-have-asked question, not
   an assertion about the world, but it is a true factual opposite.

## The limit, stated plainly

This probe does not test the design's central claim. Nothing was invented FROM the identity — the
probe produces an identity, judges it, and stops. Whether rules actually govern filling and yield
entailed content is untested by this work; a clean probe is not validation of that claim.

## How the probe actually ran (mechanism, for the record)

The generator and the criterion judge ran as stateless one-shot completions after two subagent
attempts died on provider capacity (10 retries each, `resource_exhausted`); a stateless call carries
the identical allowed inputs embedded verbatim and strictly stronger isolation — it has no tools and
cannot read any file. The scorer ran as a tool-bearing subagent restricted to the five allowed
files. The criterion judge received the twenty numbered answers only; the generator's trailing
self-assessment line inside section 12 was excluded as advocacy, per the plan's own isolation
reasoning ("an answer must be world-specific on its own face"). No seat output cites anything
outside its allowed set; the generator's output contains no tier-2/3 material.

## Recommendation on the fill mechanism — the founder's decision

- **Keep** — run the design's fill mechanism as specified, next round. The probe's evidence points
  here: the identity is world-specific, the bargain generates the pressures, and the two inference
  gaps (no-magic, diagnostic uncertainty) are prompt-visible defects a fill round would surface
  cheaply. **Recommended.**
- **Batch** — fold the fill into fewer, larger calls before testing it. Premature on this evidence:
  the multiplier is now measured (11 rules, 3 generative) but per-call cost is not, so there is no
  number to batch against.
- **Rebuild** — redesign the pass first. Nothing found here justifies it: the failures are two
  missed protective rules and one tonal misread, not a structural failure of bargain, faces, or the
  twenty functions.
