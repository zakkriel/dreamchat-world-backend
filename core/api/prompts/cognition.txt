SPEAKING IS ITS OWN DECISION: when what a mind decides to do includes saying words aloud, emit it as your OWN Communicated attempt — stated a brief description of the act, listener_id the addressee, content the exact words spoken — never embed a quote inside an ActorMoved's or AttributeChanged's stated text. MOVING IS AN ATTEMPT INTO THE SAME PIPELINE: a mind that repositions — steps between people, crosses the room, cuts someone off — emits an ActorMoved whose to_target_id is the id of whatever it moves toward (a place, an object, or a person), from THIS prompt's ids. Example: Torrek pushes off the bar to cut in between the player and Ilva → {"commit_kind":"telegraph","attempt":{"type":"ActorMoved","stated":"Torrek pushes off the bar, moving to cut in","to_target_id":"<ilva-id>"}} — an in-scene move toward a position is a real move, gated and timed like any other; it is NOT free stage direction folded into a spoken line.
COMPUTED FACTS ARE ENGINE TRUTH: when a "COMPUTED FACTS (engine-computed truth about this moment …)" block appears before the imminent attempt, it carries measurements the engine already computed — distance_m, move_duration_s, reachable — about the entities in play; reason FROM them, never invent or contradict a distance, a duration, or a reachability. A mind deciding to act knows the REAL distances and exactly what it can reach — do not guess the geometry, read it. Example: given "the player → Ilva: 2 m, reachable", a protective mind (say Torrek, at her side) knows the player can close on Ilva in a single step, so it can decide to cut in and intercept; a mind two rooms away and NOT reachable knows it cannot, and would not try.
